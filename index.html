Joshua Kenneth Krohn

December 14, 1985 – September 9, 2026

Joshua Kenneth Krohn, age 40, passed away on September 9, 2026.

Born on December 14, 1985, Josh attended preschool at the Montavilla Community Center, then Menlo Park Elementary School, Floyd Light Middle School, and Neahkahnie High School. He later served in the United States Coast Guard.

Those who knew him remember a life marked by two great loves: music and Jesus.

He poured hours into producing music and singing vocals. Making melodies was his greatest hobby. The technical side of producing was not always easy for others to follow, but the work itself was where he poured himself out. He also loved worship music.

Josh was a street evangelist who introduced many people to Jesus. He studied the Bible and knew Scripture well. Faith was not a private corner of his life; it was something he carried into the open and offered to others. He loved the Lord.

He was a very tender-hearted young man. He would often walk up to someone and give them a hug simply because he knew them. Once, seeing his coach refereeing a game, he walked onto the court while the clock was running just to get a hug. He was very funny and quick with a joke. He loved playing poker. He loved to work out and spent many hours in the gym. In the last few months of his life he created a portrait of himself and was very proud of how it looked. That portrait accompanies this obituary.

He loved good food. He loved his Aunt Tree’s house and her cooking, and he loved his stepmom’s cooking, especially her pasta salad. He absolutely loved the sourdough bread and bagels his dad would make and bring to him, and he shared those with the people around him. He would ask his mom what she was making for dinner, tell her she was going to make him fat, and then ask if they were having dessert. He also treasured the many family trips to Honeyman State Park in Florence, Oregon—time in the dunes and camping with the people he loved.

Undoubtedly his daughter Bella, whom he called Princess, was his greatest joy. He taught her how to ride her bike. Then they rode together and went to the park, especially Rainbow Park. She remembers that he loved Jesus.

Josh is survived by his daughter, Bella, and Bella’s mother, Lisa; his parents, Liz and Perry Sherbaugh and Mari and Ken Krohn; his siblings Melissa Bolds, Kristian Krohn, Chelsea Krohn, Evan Krohn, Ian Krohn, Malia Tam Sing, Micah Tam Sing, Shayla Farris, and Joshua Sherbaugh; and many nieces and nephews.

When the family sat down to write these words, they asked loved ones what should be remembered. Without fail, each one said, in some way, that Josh loved Jesus. The family finds comfort in the hope of Christ—the same Lord he loved, studied, and proclaimed.

A celebration of Josh’s life may be held in the future. When that happens, information will be shared.

In memory of Joshua Kenneth Krohn
